Life Grid

A personal Magic: The Gathering life and game-state tracker for iPhone and iPad.

Track your life total, commander damage, commander tax, counters, dice rolls, and player statuses — all in a polished dark control-panel interface. Built native for iOS with SwiftUI.

Support

Having an issue or want to suggest a feature? Reach out at example@example.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Life Grid does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All game data is stored locally on your device. No analytics, no accounts, no cloud sync, no network requests.

Data Storage

All game state, preferences, and settings are stored exclusively on your device using local file storage. You can delete this data at any time by deleting the app.

Third Parties

Life Grid does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics platforms. No data is shared with any external party.

Children

Life Grid does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
