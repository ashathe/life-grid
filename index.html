Life Grid

A personal Magic: The Gathering life and game-state tracker for iPhone and iPad.

Track your life total, commander damage, commander tax, counters, dice rolls, and player statuses — all in a polished dark control-panel interface. Built native for iOS with SwiftUI.

Support

Having an issue or want to suggest a feature? Reach out at example@example.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Life Grid does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All game data is stored locally on your device.
